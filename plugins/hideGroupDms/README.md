**Hide Group DMs** (https://lynksuser.github.io/plugins/hideGroupDms)

Plugin that hides group DMs of your choosing from your channel list. 

Allows you to hide group DMs from view without having to leave it, with no indication to the other members of the chat.

**To use:** Choose the group DMs you want to hide from the plugin settings, and toggle between hidden/unhidden.

DMs will not appear again on a new message; they will stay hidden until you unhide it.

Works by lying to the local client that the DM has been deleted. DMs may occasionally "blink" back into view in the channel list, but they should immediately disappear.
